How to read it

Blobs
One per index, left to right: Dow, S&P 500, Nasdaq, Russell 2000. Each sits above its card.
Colour
Today's move from yesterday's close. Red is falling, amber is flat, green is rising.
Height
Rising indexes float up; falling ones sink. The top and bottom of the lamp are a 1% move.
Size
Bigger means a bigger move either way. A quiet index is a small blob.
Motion
How choppy the last hour was. A steady index drifts slowly and stays whole; a choppy one churns and throws off droplets.
Mood
The average move of the four: Fearful, Uneasy, Calm, Buoyant or Euphoric.
Inside
The specks inside the S&P 500 are its biggest companies. Tap the blob, its label or "Look inside" to open it: one blob per company, with the same colour, height and motion rules as the index (inside, the top and bottom of the cell are a 2.5% move: single companies move more than an index). Size is the company's market value, or its move if you switch. A white ring marks a company moving against the index. "All 500" shows every member (a few companies have two share classes).
