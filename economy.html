    How to read it

    Blob
    One group of alike things: energy, metals, farm goods, Treasury rates, and the economy's slow numbers. Tap a blob to go inside and see each member.
    Colour
    The move, on the same scale as the stock lamp: amber is flat, green rising, red falling, full at 1%. It follows the direction of the number, not whether that is good news: rising unemployment is green here, like rising oil.
    Height
    Up floats, down sinks. Inside a group the reach is 2.5%, as inside a company view.
    Size
    A bigger move is a bigger blob. There is no market value to size a price by.
    Motion
    Restless when the last hour's prints were choppy. Weekly and monthly numbers have no last hour, so they drift slowly.
    Move
    Prices: percent change on the previous close. Rates: change in basis points (10 bp counts as 1%). Unemployment: change in points (0.1 pt counts as 1%). Jobs: thousands added (150k counts as 1%). Prices, sales and spending: percent change on the previous reading.
    Dates
    Prices are delayed by about 10 minutes while their market is open, and say "closed" with the time of the last print when it is not. Weekly and monthly numbers carry the date of the reading itself, and are never called live.
    A group's move
    The plain average of its members' moves. A group with market prices averages only those; the economy blob averages its slow numbers, each at its own date.

    Data: Yahoo Finance and FRED.
